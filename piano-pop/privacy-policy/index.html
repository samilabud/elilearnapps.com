Privacy Policy — Piano Pop

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Leer en español

Piano Pop is a music game made by EliLearn Apps for young children. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does and does not do with information.

We do not collect personal information

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no name or email field, and no way to type free text. It does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal information about you or your child.

We do not collect or use:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or photographs
  • Location
  • Contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera
  • Advertising identifiers or any other persistent device identifier for advertising

What the app stores, and where

The app saves a very small amount of information on your device only, so that your child does not lose their progress:

  • The best star rating earned on each song
  • The language you choose (English or Spanish)

This stays in the app’s private storage on the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. It is never sent anywhere.

Nothing leaves the device

Piano Pop does not connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, and no other third-party services, and it does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

Sound and vibration

The app plays musical notes through the device speaker and uses the device’s light vibration feedback when a bubble is tapped. It never uses the microphone.

Purchases

Piano Pop is a one-time paid download. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising of any kind.

No links out of the app

The app contains no links to websites, social media, or other apps that a child can reach.

Children’s privacy

Piano Pop is designed for children and is intended to comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Google Play’s Families policy, and the App Store’s requirements for apps in the Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date at the top. If a change ever means the app begins collecting information it does not collect today, we will say so clearly rather than quietly editing the text.

Contact
